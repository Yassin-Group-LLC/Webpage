How we work

Every engagement is scoped in writing before work begins. We test only systems we own or have explicit written authorization to test.

Malware samples are handled exclusively in isolated analysis environments, never on production or client networks.

Findings arrive as finished deliverables: reports, detection content, and remediation guidance mapped to the controls your auditors will ask about.
